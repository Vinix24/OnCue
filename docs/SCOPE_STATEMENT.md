# Scope Statement OnCue Recruitment mode

**Status:** Public 0.9.0
**Date:** 2026-07-22
**Author:** Vincent van Deth

---

## §1 What OnCue does

OnCue is a local AI tool that listens along during conversations and supports the conversation partner with real-time information and post-conversation documentation.

In recruitment mode this concretely means:

**Real-time transcription** via Whisper (runs locally on the machine). Audio does not leave the machine.

**Pain point, buying signal, and objection detection** based on redacted transcript fragments. The recruitment preset detects seven categories: role fit, salary gap, progression uncertainty, location/hybrid preference, doubts about the employer, contract duration, and secondary employment benefits.

**Post-conversation summary**: a structured overview of the detected themes, including timestamps within the conversation.

**Audit trail in NDJSON format**: an append-only log of all detection events, including metadata about timing and classification source. This is the basis for GDPR accountability and access requests.

---

## §2 What OnCue does NOT do

These are hard boundaries, secured in the architecture and demonstrable via code review:

- **No automatic scoring or ranking of candidates.** The system generates no numeric or categorical scores about candidates.
- **No automatic fit statements.** Text such as "suitable candidate" or "not suitable" is not generated by the system.
- **No biometric identification without opt-in.** Speaker enrollment for diarization is disabled by default and requires explicit activation per conversation.
- **No go/no-go decisions.** The system makes no decisions about hiring, rejection, or forwarding of candidates.
- **No export to third parties without an explicit user action.** Session data stays local. There is no automatic synchronization to CRM, ATS, or other external systems.
- **No training on customer data without a DPA.** The open-source version does not train on conversations. The Pro version does not use conversations for model training without an explicit data processing agreement.
- **No central storage of audio or full transcripts.** With self-hosted use, as the vendor I see no customer data whatsoever.

## §3 GDPR position

OnCue is software that runs locally at the recruiter. The **data controller** is the user: the recruiter or the recruitment agency. They determine the purpose and means of processing and are responsible for GDPR compliance.

With self-hosted use, as the vendor I am not a processor. The software is a technical aid with no access to the recruiter's data.

With **Pro tier including central audit logging** I (Vincent van Deth) am a processor under GDPR Art. 28. In that case I make a data processing agreement available. Request it via `info@vincentvandeth.nl`.

Recruitment conversations may contain special categories of personal data under GDPR Art. 9 (information about health, religion, origin that comes up in an intake conversation). The legal basis for processing special categories is the candidate's explicit consent. See `docs/DPIA_RECRUITMENT.md` §8 for the consent script.

## §4 AI Act position

As of 2 August 2026, EU AI Act Annex III classifies recruitment tools as high-risk when they evaluate, score, or rank candidates. OnCue recruitment mode is designed to fall outside that definition, on three grounds:

1. **No evaluation output.** The system generates only assistive notes and summaries, no scores or recommendations about hiring or rejection.
2. **Human-in-the-loop is structural.** The recruiter retains all decision power (aid position of Art. 14 AI Act, human oversight). The system presents information; it makes no decisions.
3. **Purpose limitation is documentation.** The primary function is conversation support and record-keeping, not selection.

A formal declaration of conformity under AI Act Annex IV is planned for the sprint+1 release. Until then, `docs/DPIA_RECRUITMENT.md` documents the risk analysis and measures that secure the aid position.

## §5 Responsibility and liability

The user is ultimately responsible for all decisions made based on OnCue output. The system supports, it does not decide.

The **open-source version** is provided "as-is" without warranties under the AGPL-3.0 license. I provide no legal warranty about the GDPR compliance of a specific deployment.

The **Pro tier** has a support SLA and a data processing agreement. Software defects reported in writing are assessed within 30 days and, if reproducible, resolved in the next release.
