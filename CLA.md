## How to sign

Contributions to this repository are accepted only under this Agreement.

**Individuals** signal agreement by adding a sign-off line to each pull request. By
adding the sign-off, You confirm that You have read and agree to Part A of this
Agreement:

```text
Signed-off-by: <Your Name> <your@email.com>
I have read and agree to the Individual CLA in CLA.md.
```

**Corporations** signal agreement by having an authorized signatory send the
completed Part B (including Schedule A) to the Owner in writing before their
employees' Contributions are merged. Contact:

- Email: info@vincentvandeth.nl
- Web: https://oncueassistant.com

The Owner records accepted Corporate CLAs and their Schedule A out of band. Once a
Corporate CLA is on file, individual contributors listed in Schedule A do not also
need to sign Part A for Contributions made on the Corporation's behalf.
